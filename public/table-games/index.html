How it works

Take a seat. The game does the rest.

1

Grab a coffee and a card

Each card is the title of one of our songs. It is your name for the game, no need to share your real one.

2

Scan the QR code

Your phone becomes the controller. No app, no sign-up. Play in English or your native language.

3

Play for 60–80 minutes

The tablet runs the rounds, a host helps out, the speaker plays KuKu'Ku music.

4

Stay in touch

Liked the company? Leave your contacts and we will seat you together again.

The games

Fun facts

Truth or myth?

Three facts about the place you are in, the city around it or the coffee in your cup. One of them is made up. Argue it out, vote on your phones and see the answer. Locals get surprised as often as newcomers.

For the laughs

Draw → phrase

Your neighbour draws your phrase, the next player describes the drawing, and round it goes. At the end you see what your phrase turned into. Every phrase is translated into each player's language, so mixed tables play together.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can I come alone?

Yes, that is the whole idea. Everyone else is there to meet new people too.

Is it a dating thing?

No. We bring together a group, not couples, and the games never ask about relationships.

I am not very talkative. Will it be awkward?

You answer on your phone and the game gives you something to talk about. Talk as much or as little as you like.

What language is the game in?

Everyone picks their language on their phone: English or their native language. At the table, speak whatever works for you.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Scan the QR code on the table and the game opens in your browser.

How long does it take?

60–80 minutes. You can leave earlier and the game carries on without you.

What happens to my data?

In the game you are a song title. Answers are anonymous and deleted after the game. We keep your contacts only if you leave them at the end.
